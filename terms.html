Kashrut - what we claim and what we do not

Every recipe on this site was written to kosher rules: no pork, no shellfish, only fish with fins and scales, and no recipe mixes meat with dairy. Each recipe is marked Meat, Dairy or Parve. Jachnun, kubaneh and malawach are built on samneh - clarified butter - so they are dairy and do not belong beside the Shabbat meat soup. Where oil can replace the samneh to make them parve, the recipe says so.

What we do not claim: this site carries no rabbinical certification and no kashrut supervision of any kind. It describes recipes; it does not grant a hechsher. The kashrut of the ingredients themselves depends entirely on the supervision marked on the products you buy - samneh, butter, meat and gelatine in particular. If you keep kosher strictly, check the products and, where it matters to you, ask your own rabbinical authority.
