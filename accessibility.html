הצהרת נגישות

עודכן לאחרונה: 29 באוגוסט 2026

Accessibility here is treated as something worth doing, not a box to tick. Adjustments were made across the site, and the results - including what was not tested - are set out below.

What was done

  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clear focus indicator on every link and button.
  • A 'skip to main content' link on every page, revealed on keyboard focus.
  • Colour contrast at AA level (4.5:1 or better) for all text.
  • Alt text on every image.
  • Correct heading hierarchy and landmark regions.
  • Hebrew and English are both fully available, including these pages, and the choice is remembered.
  • Reduced-motion preference is honoured.

What has not been tested - stated honestly

Automated testing covers only part of the accessibility requirements. Not done: testing with an actual screen reader; a full manual keyboard walkthrough; review by a certified accessibility professional; usability testing with disabled users.

Accordingly, the site applies adjustments in the spirit of WCAG 2.1 level AA - the basis for Israeli standard 5568 - but we do not state that it 'conforms to the standard', because that requires a certified accessibility professional.

Found a problem?

If anything here was hard to use, we would like to hear about it and fix it.
