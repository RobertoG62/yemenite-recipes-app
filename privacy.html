This is a static recipe site. It does not collect personal information about you: no forms, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising pixels. Every file loads from this site's own server - there are no third-party requests.

Who runs this site

A personal content project by Roberto Goldreer. No commerce, no sales, no lead collection.

Privacy contact: robertog1692@gmail.com

What this site does not collect

  • No forms, no input fields and no registration.
  • No user accounts and no passwords.
  • No cookies of any kind.
  • No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no other analytics tool.
  • No advertising networks, and no sale or sharing of data with third parties.

What does happen - stated plainly

Language preference. The site remembers whether you are reading in Hebrew or English, in your browser's localStorage under the key 'lang'. It holds one value: 'he' or 'en'. It never leaves your browser, it is not personal information, and it is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub records requests - including your IP address, browser type and time - to serve the site, secure it and prevent abuse. That logging is done by GitHub, not by us, and we have no access to those logs.

No third-party resources. Fonts, stylesheets, icons and scripts are all stored here and are not loaded from services such as Google Fonts or a CDN. Simply browsing this site does not expose your IP address to any company beyond the host.

Sharing a shopping list. Some recipes offer a 'send by WhatsApp' button. It simply opens WhatsApp with the ingredient list ready - nothing is sent to us, and we do not see who you shared it with.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is no record to retrieve, correct or delete. If you still have a question, write to the address above.

This document describes how the site actually behaves as of the date above, and is written for transparency. It is not legal advice.
